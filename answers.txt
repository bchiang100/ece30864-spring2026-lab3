PART A:
Agile development methods would allow software developers to receive more customer feedback, allowing them to make sure customers are satisfied with the ongoing product at any time.
A problem, however, is that software developers would have to change things on the fly, which would mean that upfront planning would be less feasible and a lot of the work would be decided after each Sprint Review.

PART B:
The scrum master is more concerned with team dynamics and removing blockers from developers. The product owner is the one that defines the product vision, values, and requirements from stakeholders.

Part C:
The three types are meetings are Sprint Planning (settings requirements and goals), Daily Scrum (15 minute meetings for updates), and Sprint Review (work demoing).

Sprint Planning: Discusses goals and reviews product backlog.

Daily Scrum: Discussed what they did since the last meeting and identifying blockers.

Sprint Review: Demoes work and adjusts backlog based on new needs and requirements.\

Part D:
Epics are high level workloads that span multiple Sprints. Stories represent tasks to be completed within a single Sprint. Lastly, a task represent each feature that needs to be completed. Epics can contain multiple stories which can contain multiple tasks.
