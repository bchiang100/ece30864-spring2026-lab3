PART A:
Agile development methods would allow software developers to receive more customer feedback, allowing them to make sure customers are satisfied with the ongoing product at any time.
A problem, however, is that software developers would have to change things on the fly, which would mean that upfront planning would be less feasible and a lot of the work would be decided after each Sprint Review.

PART B:
The scrum master is more concerned with team dynamics and removing blockers from developers. The product owner is the one that defines the product vision, values, and requirements from stakeholders.
